# CT broadbeam throughput campaign — 2026-10-05

The two CT broadbeam defaults now use energy/density ordering and selected-tissue electron survival 0.5. On RTX 2080 Ti, matched resident throughput improves **164,029→201,434 primary/s (+22.8%) for proton** and **103,325→126,102 (+22.0%) for carbon**. Both pass the required dose gate. **300k/s has not been reached.**

Each seed passes separately: proton selected 99.9283/99.9355%; carbon 99.8094/99.8529%. Pooling reduces Monte Carlo noise and changes gamma in both arms; the matched pooled comparison shows no material regression. Pooled full-mask dose-sum bias is proton −0.03654→−0.03669% and carbon −0.49836→−0.49839%, without fitted normalization. Machine-readable timings, all gates and regional statistics are in [broadbeam_300k_20261005.json](broadbeam_300k_20261005.json).

Baseline: master `1813ba0`, RTX 2080 Ti, FP32 dose ON / FP64 dose OFF, native repeated batches with packages retained in one process. Proton RT07575 uses 1,253,150 primary per batch; carbon uses 925,987. Resident throughput excludes the first batch; whole-process throughput includes loading and output. GPU jobs are serialized. Automatic GPU clocks are retained and telemetry is recorded; tiny timing differences are not treated as gains.

The dose gate is absolute, full-reference-grid >=10% of maximum, **3D local 1%/1 mm >99%**, Cartesian spherical search step **0.1 mm**, unchanged normalization and coordinate mapping. Both arms retain the 5 mm selected halo, group32, 0.5 low-density survival, full electron/photon continuation, FP32 dose, coverage, energy, queue and completion audits. The gamma references and histories are recorded in each run's `gamma.json` and `execution.json`.

## Candidates

1. **Order independent electron task indices by sampled birth energy and physical density.** Four density bands and eight energy bands plus an invalid bucket; histogram, prefix sum and scatter precede the existing dynamic worker. The ordering reads the exact stored birth draw and consumes no RNG. Payloads and physical histories are unchanged. Extra storage is five bytes per queue capacity plus small bucket arrays (~27.7 MiB at the 576 MiB task budget). Index indirection is compiled out of minibeam workers. An explicit YAML switch permits comparisons. Final same-binary sorting-only screens improve resident rate 4.43% proton / 4.06% carbon; all compared work is identical and dose relative L1 is at most 5.00e-8.
2. **Selected tissue birth roulette.** Above the existing density threshold 0.1 g/cm³, optionally retain a fraction of tasks and transport survivors with weight W/p. Their photon descendants retain the weight, and the signed roulette ledger closes energy accounting. This changes variance, not transport laws; it is not local deposition or a proof that electrons cannot cross an interface. Existing low-density roulette stays at 0.5. Survival 1 disables the new estimator. No research environment override is retained in production.
3. **Rejected queue compaction 104→72 bytes.** Both species retain exact work and dose but show no useful timing improvement.
4. **Rejected identical density-node query reuse.** Exact-work screens pass but neither electron nor whole-run time improves.
5. **Rejected EM counter shards 64→1024.** Exact totals and dose pass; no useful performance gain.

Two-seed, three-batch tissue-only screens gave proton 167.6→197.0k/s at p=0.5 and 218.0k/s at p=0.25; carbon 105.3→124.0k/s and 136.5k/s. The more aggressive p=0.25 worsens carbon same-time internal-air and skin precision (efficiency ratios 0.879 and 0.799), so it is not selected as a default. A passing gamma on a noisier evaluated dose is not evidence of improved accuracy.

## Hardware evidence

Separate Nsight Compute profiling of the ordering research binary (first electron launch of the RT07575 proton plan, OFF versus energy/density plus kill-bucket mode2) measured active threads per instruction 12.0856→12.9492, warp instructions 46.056→42.982 billion, essentially unchanged executed thread instructions 556.622→556.590 billion, local-load sectors 8.620→8.167 billion and local-store sectors 6.624→6.336 billion. Registers remain 255 and active-warp occupancy approximately 24.85%. This supports reduced divergence/local traffic, not fewer physical interactions or an occupancy gain. Profiled kernel time 1.9142→1.6981 s is **not** whole-run throughput. Workload-label correction: the old profiler command requested `--histories 100000`, but TPS history weights retained the full12,531,500-history plan, with524,288-primary chunks. Only one matching electron launch was captured. The profiler counters remain a matched comparison, but must not be labelled a100k-primary run; native-batch timing/validation elsewhere is unaffected.

Mode2 has no resolved whole-run benefit over simpler energy/density-only mode1, so only mode1 is integrated. Do not attribute the exact mode2 hardware numbers to the final mode1 binary.

## Configuration and the remaining 300k/s gap

The two CT broadbeam YAMLs select:

```yaml
broadbeam_electron_birth_order: true
broadbeam_electron_tissue_survival: 0.5
```

Set tissue survival to 1 to disable the new roulette; set birth ordering to false to restore original task order. Tracking OFF resets both controls. Inputs must remain finite with survival in (0,1]. Water shared tracking and minibeam receive neither optimization; no research environment-variable override remains in production.

Mean resident kernel times (seconds per batch):

| Species | Primary control → selected | Secondary control → selected | Electron control → selected |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Proton | 3.300→3.313 | 0.271→0.264 | **3.721→2.293** |
| Carbon | 1.703→1.708 | 2.829→2.832 | **4.171→2.553** |

Proton's 1,253,150-primary batch must fall from 6.221 to 4.177 s to reach 300k/s (another 32.9% time reduction). The measured non-electron remainder is 3.928 s, leaving only 0.249 s for electrons if other work is unchanged. Carbon's 925,987-primary batch must fall from 7.343 to 3.087 s (another 58.0%); its non-electron remainder alone is 4.791 s. Thus ion transport and its memory/state costs must be reduced too, especially carbon secondary transport. Hypothetically removing electron cost gives about 319k/s proton and 193k/s carbon under this fixed-other-cost calculation; these are budget diagnostics, **not** attainable-rate promises or fundamental hardware limits. No water speed claim is made.

## Evidence and reproducibility

`out/broadbeam_300k_20261005/` contains frozen control, isolated candidate patches, driver scripts, commands, configs, batch outputs, audit/quality/energy reports, GPU telemetry and profiler reports. `validate_final.py` checks the final compiled OFF path against the frozen control, checks sorting-only exact-work equivalence, then runs two independent seeds with reversed arm order, 10 batches per arm/seed for proton and 7 for carbon. `tissue_noise.py` computes regional two-seed RMS differences and efficiency (rate/noise²) using fixed masks. This is a finite two-seed diagnostic, not a formal confidence bound. `regress_unchanged.py` covers the other six presets. `failed.md` records discarded physics/performance routes and rejected harness attempts.
