# Compact-only minibeam pipeline (2026-10-07)

Historical implementation record: the later cleanup removed the redundant
`minibeam_primary_pipeline` key; current minibeams always use compact scheduling.

All four minibeams now use the Cu/air launch followed by a medium launch.
Carbon water keeps its TPS CSV source, source sampling/emittance, spot allocation,
per-spot seeds and generic Urban water medium kernel. Its 64-byte entry record
preserves history/RNG identity, sampled energy, position and direction.
The monolithic stage-0 minibeam kernel dispatch and its instantiation are removed.
`minibeam_primary_pipeline` accepts only `compact`, including when omitted.
Broadbeam still dispatches its existing kernels without medium-entry buffers.
Ion physics, packages, step caps, fluctuations and FP32 dose are unchanged.
