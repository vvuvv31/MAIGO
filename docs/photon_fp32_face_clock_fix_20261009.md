# FP32 photon face-clock correction (2026-10-09)

The carbon HU0 campaign stopped at rep23/chunk76 after 2376 accepted batches (287.496M incident ions). The original failed batch was rejected and had no complete.json or accepted dose. GPU was not resumed promptly during the TOPAS recovery; this was an operational omission, separate from the transport defect.

The same batch and primary/descendant seeds reproduced the failure. The live audit now records an explicit photon failure reason and FP32 before/after position/direction. Reason3 is invalid directed-cell ownership on re-entry: a photon started at (0.530400276,13.6407509,58.9928551)mm with direction(0.873296559,0.0395948626,-0.485577345) and a subsequent interaction was placed at x=40.0000076mm, outside the represented x=40mm phantom boundary. The voxel navigator accumulated next-face clocks by repeatedly adding spacing/abs(direction). FP32 rounding displaced the accumulated outer-plane clock from the actual represented plane. The regression counterexample accumulates approximately3.66e−5mm outward drift over the x-plane traversal. A near-exit interaction could therefore occur outside the actual CT while still preceding the accumulated navigator face; the next photon flight correctly rejected the invalid point.

The correction in include/carbon/electron_fp32.hpp recomputes each next-face clock from the current represented cell plane and original ray origin after crossing. Geometry, loss and dose remain FP32; packages, conditional outcomes, cutoffs, source weights, seeds and nuclear neutral sinks are unchanged. No tolerance enlargement, point snapping, missing-data deposition or neutral transport was added. The added failure reasons remain part of existing runtime audits rather than a diagnostic build mode.
